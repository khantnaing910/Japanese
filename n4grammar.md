# 📙 JLPT N4 Grammar Guide
### Minna no Nihongo Lessons 26–50 — Complete Reference

---

## 💡 Quick Tips: How to Ace N4 Grammar Questions

> **🎯 Tip 1: Identify the sentence pattern first.**
> Before looking at the choices, read the full sentence and identify what grammar point is being tested. Look for keywords like のに, ために, ように — these are strong pattern indicators.

> **🎯 Tip 2: Pay attention to verb forms.**
> N4 grammar heavily depends on which verb form comes before the grammar point (dictionary form, た-form, ない-form, て-form, etc.). Getting this wrong = getting the answer wrong.

> **🎯 Tip 3: Watch for particles.**
> Many wrong answers differ by just one particle (を vs が vs に). Train yourself to notice which particle each grammar pattern requires.

> **🎯 Tip 4: Use elimination.**
> If you're unsure, eliminate options that are grammatically impossible (e.g., wrong verb form) before guessing.

> **🎯 Tip 5: Context matters.**
> Many grammar points have overlapping meanings. The surrounding context (formal/informal, positive/negative, speaker's intention) determines the correct one.

## 📖 Lesson 26 — ～んです (Explanation/Emphasis)

### Grammar 1: ～んです / ～のです

**Meaning:** Used to explain a situation, give a reason, or emphasize.

**Formation:**
| Part of Speech | How to Connect |
|---|---|
| Verb (casual) | 行く**んです** |
| い-Adj | 高い**んです** |
| な-Adj | 静か**なんです** |
| Noun | 学生**なんです** |

**Examples:**
- あしたから旅行**なんです**。 → I'm going on a trip starting tomorrow. *(explaining)*
- どうして遅れた**んですか**。 → Why were you late? *(seeking explanation)*

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** Use ～んですが when you're leading into a request or asking for advice. It softens the tone.
> Example: いい先生を紹介して**いただけませんか**。

---

### Grammar 2: ～ていただけませんか (Polite Request)

**Meaning:** Could you please do ~ for me? (Very polite)

**Formation:** Verb て-form + いただけませんか

**Examples:**
- 先生を紹介して**いただけませんか**。 → Could you please introduce me to a teacher?

> 💡 **Politeness Scale:**
> ～てください < ～てくれませんか < ～てもらえませんか < ～ていただけませんか (most polite)

---

## 📖 Lesson 27 — Potential Form (可能形)

### Grammar 1: Potential Verbs (～can do)

**Meaning:** To express ability — "can do ~"

**Formation:**

| Group | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Group I | う-ending → え-ending + る | 話す → 話**せる** |
| Group II | る → **られる** | 食べる → 食べ**られる** |
| Group III | する → **できる**, 来る → **来られる** | — |

**Examples:**
- 日本語が少し**話せます**。 → I can speak a little Japanese.
- 山が**はっきり見えます**。 → The mountains are clearly visible.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** 見える (can see) and 聞こえる (can hear) are NOT potential forms — they're separate verbs describing involuntary perception. Don't confuse with 見られる/聞ける!

---

### Grammar 2: ～できます (Something is built/completed)

**Meaning:** Something new has been created/built

**Example:**
- 駅の前に大きいスーパーが**できました**。 → A big supermarket was built in front of the station.

---

## 📖 Lesson 28 — ～ながら / ～し / Habitual Actions

### Grammar 1: V(ます-stem) + ながら (While doing ~)

**Meaning:** Doing two actions simultaneously. The main action is the second verb.

**Examples:**
- 音楽を聞き**ながら**食事します。 → I eat while listening to music.
- テレビを見**ながら**勉強しています。 → I study while watching TV.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** The subject must be the same for both actions. You can't say "While I eat, he studies" with ながら.

---

### Grammar 2: ～ています (Habitual Action)

**Meaning:** Describes a habitual/repeated action

**Example:**
- 毎朝ジョギングを**しています**。 → I jog every morning. *(habitual)*

---

### Grammar 3: ～し、～し (Listing Reasons)

**Meaning:** Gives multiple reasons — "~ and also ~, so..."

**Formation:** [Clause 1] し、[Clause 2] し、[Result]

**Example:**
- 地下鉄は速い**し**、安い**し**、地下鉄で行きましょう。
  → The subway is fast AND cheap, so let's take the subway.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** ～し is great for listing multiple reasons to justify a conclusion. In the JLPT, if you see a sentence with two or more reasons followed by a suggestion, ～し is likely the answer.

---

## 📖 Lesson 29 — ～ています (Resultant State) / ～てしまいました

### Grammar 1: ～ています (State resulting from an action)

**Meaning:** Describes a current state that resulted from a past action.

**Examples:**
- 窓が閉まっ**ています**。 → The window is closed. *(It was closed and remains so.)*
- 電気がつい**ています**。 → The light is on.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** This is different from progressive ～ています (doing something now). Context tells you which meaning:
> - 食べ**ています** → is eating (progressive)
> - 結婚し**ています** → is married (state)

---

### Grammar 2: ～てしまいました (Completion / Regret)

**Meaning:** (1) Something was completed, or (2) Something unfortunate/regrettable happened.

**Examples:**
- 電車に傘を忘れ**てしまいました**。 → I accidentally left my umbrella on the train. *(regret)*
- 宿題を全部やっ**てしまいました**。 → I've finished all my homework. *(completion)*

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** In casual speech, ～てしまう becomes ～ちゃう (Group II) or ～じゃう (Group I):
> 忘れてしまった → 忘れ**ちゃった**

---

## 📖 Lesson 30 — ～てあります / ～ておきます

### Grammar 1: ～てあります (Something has been done intentionally)

**Meaning:** Describes a state resulting from someone's intentional action.

**Formation:** Transitive Verb て-form + あります

**Example:**
- 交番に町の地図がはっ**てあります**。 → A town map has been posted at the police box.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** ～ています (intransitive) vs ～てあります (transitive):
> - 窓が開い**ています** → The window is open. *(just describing)*
> - 窓が開け**てあります** → The window has been opened. *(someone opened it on purpose)*

---

### Grammar 2: ～ておきます (Preparation / Leaving as is)

**Meaning:** To do something in advance (preparation), or to leave something as it is.

**Examples:**
- 旅行の前に調べ**ておきます**。 → I'll look it up before the trip. *(preparation)*
- そのままにし**ておいてください**。 → Please leave it as it is.

---

## 📖 Lesson 31 — Volitional Form (意向形)

### Grammar 1: ～（よ）う (Let's ~ / I'll ~)

**Meaning:** Casual suggestion or personal intention.

**Formation:**

| Group | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Group I | う-ending → おう | 行く → 行**こう** |
| Group II | る → **よう** | 食べる → 食べ**よう** |
| Group III | する → **しよう**, 来る → **来よう** | — |

**Example:**
- いっしょに行**こう**。 → Let's go together.

---

### Grammar 2: Volitional + と思っています (Intending to ~)

**Meaning:** I'm thinking of doing ~

**Example:**
- 将来自分の会社を作**ろうと思っています**。 → I'm thinking of starting my own company in the future.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** ～と思っています (ongoing intention) vs ～と思います (just decided):
> - 行こう**と思っています** → I've been planning to go.
> - 行こう**と思います** → I think I'll go. (decided now)

---

### Grammar 3: ～つもりです (Plan to ~)

**Meaning:** I plan to / I intend to

**Example:**
- 来月車を買う**つもりです**。 → I plan to buy a car next month.
- あの映画は見ない**つもりです**。 → I don't plan to see that movie.

---

## 📖 Lesson 32 — Advice & Probability

### Grammar 1: ～たほうがいい (Should / Had better)

**Meaning:** It would be better to ~ / You should ~

**Formation:**
- Verb た-form + ほうがいい *(positive advice)*
- Verb ない-form + ほうがいい *(negative advice)*

**Examples:**
- 毎日運動し**たほうがいいです**。 → You should exercise every day.
- 無理をし**ないほうがいいですよ**。 → You'd better not overdo it.

---

### Grammar 2: ～でしょう (Probably)

**Meaning:** Probably / I think ~ (conjecture)

**Example:**
- あしたは雪が降る**でしょう**。 → It will probably snow tomorrow.

---

### Grammar 3: ～かもしれません (Might / Maybe)

**Meaning:** It might be ~ / Maybe ~

**Example:**
- 約束の時間に間に合わない**かもしれません**。 → I might not make it on time.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** Certainty scale:
> ～かもしれません (maybe ~30%) < ～でしょう (probably ~70%) < ～はずです (should be ~90%)

---

## 📖 Lesson 33 — Imperative & Quotation

### Grammar 1: Imperative Form (命令形) — ～ろ / ～え

**Meaning:** Do ~! (Direct command)

| Group | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Group I | う-ending → え | 急ぐ → 急**げ** |
| Group II | る → **ろ** | 食べる → 食べ**ろ** |
| Group III | する → **しろ**, 来る → **来い** | — |

**Example:**
- 急**げ**。 → Hurry up!

---

### Grammar 2: ～な (Prohibition — Don't ~!)

**Meaning:** Don't do ~! (Casual/strong prohibition)

**Formation:** Verb dictionary form + な

**Example:**
- 触る**な**。 → Don't touch!

---

### Grammar 3: ～という意味です (Means that ~)

**Meaning:** It means ~

**Example:**
- 立ち入り禁止は入る**なという意味です**。 → "No entry" means "Don't enter."

---

### Grammar 4: ～と言っていました (Reported Speech)

**Meaning:** (Someone) said that ~

**Example:**
- ミラーさんは来週大阪へ出張する**と言っていました**。 → Mr. Miller said he'll go to Osaka on a business trip next week.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** Use casual form before と言っていました. The verb tense inside the quote stays as the original speaker said it.

---

## 📖 Lesson 34 — ～とおりに / ～あとで / ～ないで

### Grammar 1: ～とおりに (Do exactly as ~)

**Meaning:** In the same way as ~ / Just as ~

**Examples:**
- 先生が言った**とおりに**書きます。 → I'll write exactly as the teacher said.
- わたしがした**とおりに**してください。 → Please do it the way I did.

---

### Grammar 2: ～たあとで (After doing ~)

**Meaning:** After ~

**Example:**
- ごはんを食べた**あとで**歯を磨きます。 → After eating, I brush my teeth.

---

### Grammar 3: ～ないで (Without doing ~)

**Meaning:** Without ~ / Instead of ~

**Example:**
- コーヒーは砂糖を入れ**ないで**飲みます。 → I drink coffee without sugar.

---

## 📖 Lesson 35 — Conditional Forms (ば / なら)

### Grammar 1: ～ば (If ~)

**Meaning:** If ~ (general/natural condition)

**Formation:**

| Group | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Group I | う → え + ば | 行く → 行**けば** |
| Group II | る → れ + ば | 食べる → 食べ**れば** |
| い-Adj | い → け + れば | 高い → 高**ければ** |
| な-Adj / N | + であれば / なら | 静か**なら** |

**Examples:**
- 春に**なれば**桜が咲きます。 → If spring comes, the cherry blossoms will bloom.
- 天気が**よければ**島が見えます。 → If the weather is good, you can see the island.

---

### Grammar 2: ～なら (If it's the case that ~)

**Meaning:** If (it's about) ~ / If you're talking about ~

**Example:**
- 北海道旅行**なら**6月がいいです。 → If it's a Hokkaido trip, June is best.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** Conditional summary:
> - **～ば** → General/natural conditions
> - **～たら** → "When/if" (more casual, any situation)
> - **～なら** → "If it's about ~" (topic-based advice)
> - **～と** → Automatic/habitual results ("whenever")

---

## 📖 Lesson 36 — ～ように (Purpose / Effort / Change)

### Grammar 1: ～ように (In order to / So that ~)

**Meaning:** Purpose with non-volitional or potential verbs

**Example:**
- 速く泳げる**ように**毎日練習しています。 → I practice every day so I can swim fast.

---

### Grammar 2: ～ようになりました (Has come to ~)

**Meaning:** A change in ability or habit

**Example:**
- やっと自転車に乗れる**ようになりました**。 → I finally became able to ride a bicycle.

---

### Grammar 3: ～ようにしています (Try to / Make a habit of ~)

**Meaning:** I make an effort to ~ / I try to ~

**Example:**
- 毎日日記を書く**ようにしています**。 → I make it a habit to write in my diary every day.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** ために vs ように:
> - **ために** → with volitional verbs (自分の店を持つ**ために**貯金する)
> - **ように** → with non-volitional/potential verbs (泳げる**ように**練習する)

---

## 📖 Lesson 37 — Passive Form (受身形)

### Grammar: Verb Passive Form

**Meaning:** To be ~ed (something is done to you)

**Formation:**

| Group | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Group I | う → あ + れる | 読む → 読**まれる** |
| Group II | る → **られる** | 食べる → 食べ**られる** |
| Group III | する → **される**, 来る → **来られる** | — |

**Examples:**
- 子どものとき母に**しかられました**。 → When I was a child, I was scolded by my mother.
- 電車で足を**踏まれました**。 → My foot was stepped on in the train. *(nuisance passive)*
- 法隆寺は607年に**建てられました**。 → Horyuji was built in 607. *(objective passive)*

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** N4 passive has 3 uses:
> 1. **Direct passive:** 先生にほめ**られた** (was praised by teacher)
> 2. **Nuisance passive:** 雨に降**られた** (got rained on — annoying!)
> 3. **Objective:** この本は世界中で読**まれている** (is read worldwide)

## 📖 Lesson 38 — Verb Nominalization (～の)

### Grammar: V(dictionary) + の + は/が/を

**Meaning:** Turns a verb phrase into a noun — "doing ~"

**Examples:**
- 絵をかく**のは**楽しいです。 → Drawing is fun.
- 星を見る**のが**好きです。 → I like watching stars.
- 財布を持って来る**のを**忘れました。 → I forgot to bring my wallet.
- 日本へ来た**のは**去年の3月です。 → It was March of last year that I came to Japan.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** の replaces こと in casual/spoken Japanese. Both nominalize verbs, but の sounds more natural in conversation.

## 📖 Lesson 39 — Reasons (～て / ～で / ～ので)

### Grammar 1: ～て (Cause — Emotional Response)

**Meaning:** Because of ~ (leading to emotion/involuntary response)

**Example:**
- ニュースを聞い**て**びっくりしました。 → I was surprised to hear the news.

---

### Grammar 2: ～で (Cause — Event/Situation)

**Meaning:** Due to ~ (external cause)

**Example:**
- 地震**で**ビルが倒れました。 → The building collapsed due to the earthquake.

---

### Grammar 3: ～ので (Because ~)

**Meaning:** Because ~ (polite reason)

**Example:**
- 体の調子が悪い**ので**病院へ行きます。 → I'm going to the hospital because I feel unwell.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** ので vs から:
> - **ので** → softer, polite, objective → preferred in formal settings & JLPT
> - **から** → stronger, more assertive, subjective

---

## 📖 Lesson 40 — Embedded Questions / ～てみる

### Grammar 1: Question word + か (Embedded Question)

**Meaning:** Indirect/embedded question

**Example:**
- JL107便は何時に到着する**か**調べてください。 → Please check what time flight JL107 arrives.

---

### Grammar 2: ～かどうか (Whether or not)

**Meaning:** Whether ~ or not

**Example:**
- 台風が来る**かどうか**まだわかりません。 → I don't know yet whether the typhoon will come.

---

### Grammar 3: ～てみる (Try doing ~)

**Meaning:** To try doing ~ (to see what happens)

**Example:**
- この服を着**てみても**いいですか。 → May I try on these clothes?

## 📖 Lesson 41 — Giving & Receiving Actions (敬語)

### Grammar: いただく / くださる / やる

| Pattern | Meaning | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| ～ていただく | to have someone do ~ (humble) | ↑ superior does for me |
| ～てくださる | someone kindly does ~ (honorific) | ↑ superior does for me |
| ～てやる | to do ~ for (children/pets) | ↓ I do for someone lower |

**Examples:**
- 先生に漢字を直し**ていただきました**。 → I had the teacher correct my kanji.
- 奥さんがお茶を教え**てくださいました**。 → The wife kindly taught me tea ceremony.
- 息子に紙飛行機を作っ**てやりました**。 → I made a paper airplane for my son.

## 📖 Lesson 42 — ～ために / ～のに

### Grammar 1: ～ために (In order to / For the purpose of)

**Meaning:** Purpose (with volitional verbs)

**Example:**
- 自分の店を持つ**ために**貯金しています。 → I'm saving money in order to have my own shop.

---

### Grammar 2: ～のにいい (Good for ~ing)

**Meaning:** Good/useful for doing ~

**Example:**
- この靴は山を歩く**のにいいです**。 → These shoes are good for walking in the mountains.

## 📖 Lesson 43 — ～そうです (Appearance) / ～て来ます

### Grammar 1: ～そうです (Looks like ~ / About to ~)

**Meaning:** It looks like ~ / It seems ~ (based on appearance)

**Formation:**
- Verb ます-stem + そうです
- い-Adj (drop い) + そうです
- な-Adj + そうです

**Examples:**
- 今にも雨が降り**そうです**。 → It looks like it's about to rain.
- おいし**そうです**ね。 → It looks delicious!

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** いい → **よさ**そう (exception!)
> ない → **なさ**そう (exception!)

---

### Grammar 2: ～て来ます (Go and come back)

**Meaning:** Go do something and come back

**Example:**
- ちょっと切符を買っ**て来ます**。 → I'll go buy a ticket (and come back).

---

## 📖 Lesson 44 — ～すぎます / ～やすい・にくい / Adj→Adv

### Grammar 1: ～すぎます (Too much)

**Meaning:** Too much / Excessively

**Examples:**
- お酒を飲み**すぎました**。 → I drank too much.
- この問題は難し**すぎます**。 → This problem is too difficult.

---

### Grammar 2: ～やすい / ～にくい (Easy/Hard to ~)

**Meaning:** Easy to ~ / Hard to ~

**Examples:**
- このパソコンは使い**やすいです**。 → This computer is easy to use.
- この漢字は覚え**にくいです**。 → This kanji is hard to memorize.

---

### Grammar 3: Adjective → くします / にします (Make ~)

**Meaning:** To make something [adjective]

**Examples:**
- ズボンを短**くしてください**。 → Please make the pants shorter.
- 部屋をきれい**にしてください**。 → Please make the room clean.

---

## 📖 Lesson 45 — ～場合は / ～のに (Despite)

### Grammar 1: ～場合は (In case of ~)

**Meaning:** In the case of ~ / If ~

**Example:**
- カードをなくした**場合は**すぐカード会社に連絡してください。
  → If you lose your card, contact the card company immediately.

---

### Grammar 2: ～のに (Despite / Even though)

**Meaning:** Although ~ / Even though ~ (with surprise or frustration)

**Example:**
- 約束をした**のに**彼女は来ませんでした。 → Even though we made a promise, she didn't come.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** のに expresses the speaker's surprise, frustration, or disappointment. It's stronger than けど/が.

---

## 📖 Lesson 46 — ～ところです / ～ばかりです / ～はずです

### Grammar 1: ～ところです (About to / Just doing / Just did)

**Meaning:** Indicates timing relative to an action.

| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|
| V(dictionary) + ところです | about to do ~ |
| V(ている) + ところです | in the middle of doing ~ |
| V(た) + ところです | just finished doing ~ |

**Example:**
- 会議は今から始まる**ところです**。 → The meeting is about to start.

---

### Grammar 2: ～たばかりです (Just did ~)

**Meaning:** Just (recently) did ~

**Example:**
- 彼は3月に大学を卒業した**ばかりです**。 → He just graduated from university in March.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** ～たところ = *just this moment*; ～たばかり = *recently* (can be a wider timeframe)

---

### Grammar 3: ～はずです (Should / Expected to)

**Meaning:** It should be ~ / It is expected that ~

**Example:**
- ミラーさんは会議室にいる**はずです**。 → Mr. Miller should be in the meeting room.

---

## 📖 Lesson 47 — ～そうです (Hearsay) / ～ようです (Inference)

### Grammar 1: ～そうです (I heard that ~)

**Meaning:** Reported information — "I heard that ~"

**Formation:** Plain form + そうです

**Example:**
- 天気予報によるとあしたは寒くなる**そうです**。 → According to the weather forecast, it will get cold tomorrow.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** Two types of そうです:
> - **Appearance** (L43): 降り**そうです** → "It looks like it'll rain" *(ます-stem + そう)*
> - **Hearsay** (L47): 降る**そうです** → "I heard it'll rain" *(plain form + そう)*

---

### Grammar 2: ～ようです (It seems like ~)

**Meaning:** It appears that ~ (based on evidence/observation)

**Example:**
- 隣の部屋にだれかいる**ようです**。 → It seems like someone is in the next room.

---

## 📖 Lesson 48 — Causative Form (使役形)

### Grammar: Verb Causative Form

**Meaning:** To make/let someone do ~

**Formation:**

| Group | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Group I | う → あ + せる | 読む → 読**ませる** |
| Group II | る → **させる** | 食べる → 食べ**させる** |
| Group III | する → **させる**, 来る → **来させる** | — |

**Examples:**
- 息子をイギリスへ留学**させます**。 → I'll have my son study abroad in England. *(make/let)*
- 娘にピアノを習**わせます**。 → I'll have my daughter learn piano.

> 💡 **Quick Tip:** The particle changes based on transitivity:
> - Intransitive verb: 子どもを行かせる (AをV)
> - Transitive verb: 子どもにピアノを弾かせる (AにBをV)

## 📖 Lesson 49 — Honorific Language (尊敬語)

### Grammar: Respectful Forms

| Pattern | Usage | Example |
|---|---|---|
| V(passive form) | Simple honorific | 課長は帰**られました** |
| お + V(ます-stem) + になる | Standard honorific | 社長はお帰り**になりました** |
| Special verbs | Irregular honorific | 部長はアメリカへ出張**なさいます** |
| お + V(ます-stem) + ください | Polite request | しばらくお待ち**ください** |

**Special Honorific Verbs:**

| Normal | Honorific |
|---|---|
| 行く・来る・いる | いらっしゃる |
| 食べる・飲む | 召し上がる |
| 言う | おっしゃる |
| する | なさる |
| 見る | ご覧になる |
| 知っている | ご存じです |
| くれる | くださる |

## 📖 Lesson 50 — Humble Language (謙譲語)

### Grammar: Humble Forms

| Pattern | Usage | Example |
|---|---|---|
| お + V(ます-stem) + します | Standard humble | スケジュールをお送り**します** |
| Special verbs | Irregular humble | あした3時に**伺います** |

**Examples:**
- 今月のスケジュールを**お送りします**。 → I will send you this month's schedule.
- あした3時に**伺います**。 → I will visit at 3 o'clock tomorrow.
- 私はアメリカから**参りました**。 → I came from America.

> 💡 **Quick Tip for Keigo Questions:**
> 1. Who is the subject? → If it's "you/superior" → use **尊敬語 (honorific)**
> 2. If the subject is "I/we" → use **謙譲語 (humble)**
> 3. Never mix them up! Don't use humble for others or honorific for yourself.

## 🏆 Final Exam Tips

### ✅ Top 5 Most Tested Grammar Points:
1. **～ようにする vs ～ために** — Know when to use each!
2. **～てしまう** — Regret vs completion context
3. **Passive vs Causative** — Formation AND particle usage
4. **そうです** — Appearance (ます-stem) vs Hearsay (plain form)
5. **Conditionals** — ば vs たら vs なら vs と

### ✅ Common Traps:
- ❌ Mixing up ～ています (progressive vs state)
- ❌ Using ために with non-volitional verbs (use ように instead)
- ❌ Confusing honorific (for others) and humble (for yourself)
- ❌ Wrong particle with causative (を vs に)

1. **Read** the grammar point and understand the meaning
