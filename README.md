## 📖 About

This repository contains well-organized vocabulary, kanji, and grammar guides for the **Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT)** at **N5** (beginner), **N4** (pre-intermediate), and **N3** (intermediate) levels. All materials are based on the **Minna no Nihongo** and **Nihongo Sou Matome** textbook series, formatted in clean, easy-to-read Markdown tables — perfect for self-study, quick reference, or building your own flashcard decks.

## 📋 Sample Entries

### Vocabulary Format

| # | Japanese | Reading | Group | Meaning |
|---|----------|---------|-------|---------|
| 1 | 食べます | たべます | II | to eat |
| 2 | 飲みます | のみます | I | to drink |

### Kanji Format

| # | Kanji | Kun'yomi | On'yomi | Meaning | Key Vocabulary |
|---|-------|----------|---------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | 食 | た(べる) | ショク | Eat | 食べる (II) to eat · 食堂 dining hall |

### Grammar Format

> **Vて + ください** (Please do ~)
> - ここに名前を書い**てください**。 → Please write your name here.
> - 💡 *Quick Tip: て-form is the foundation for 10+ grammar patterns. Master it first!*
